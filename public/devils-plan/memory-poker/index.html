How to play

01 · Memorize
Study a 4×4 grid of sixteen face-up cards for 20 seconds.
02 · Turn over
The board flips face-down and three community cards are revealed.
03 · Choose two
Within 60 seconds, select two positions from memory. These two plus the shared cards form a five-card poker hand.
04 · Compare
Standard poker ranks decide the outcome. Suits do not break ties, and an exact tie is a redeal.

These are independent practice modes, not the show's 16-player survival ranking. An online 1v1 mode is not included yet.
