Rules

Each player gets rock cards 0–6, two chips each in red, green, and blue, and one rainbow chip. Everyone selects face-down together.

Rainbow chips place first, then larger rock cards. A rainbow can choose its stand when placed. You may pass once to move to the end of the order, except when already last.

When a stand reaches 13 or more, the player receives one penalty per rock already on it, even zero-valued rocks. The newly placed rock remains. Fewest penalties after seven rounds wins.

This four-player practice includes three computer opponents. Their strategy is simplified. Not a reproduction of the original site's multiplayer.
